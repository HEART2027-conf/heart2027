About HEART 2027

The International Symposium on Highly Efficient Accelerators and Reconfigurable Technologies (HEART) is a forum for presenting and discussing new research on computing systems built around acceleration technology. HEART focuses on achieving high efficiency through accelerators, specialized hardware architectures, and new design methods for computing systems — themes that span high-performance computing and data centers as well as mobile and IoT applications.

HEART 2027 will be held at Meiji University in Tokyo, Japan. Details on the venue, keynote speakers, and the Call for Papers will be posted here as they are confirmed.
